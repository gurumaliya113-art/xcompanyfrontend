💡 Foundation Philosophy

Core Principles

Six immutable rules that govern the entire FCP system

Founders Must Always Retain Control
Strategic decisions, voting power, and asset control permanently remain with founders. Minimum 40% ownership floor is immutable.
Only Profits May Be Shared
Asset ownership is never distributed. Only profits from assets may be allocated (up to 20%) through the Asset Profit Pool.
Ownership Earned Through Contribution
Participation is merit-based and performance-driven. All rewards require verified proof of work and explicit approval.
Assets Belong 100% to Company
No employee or participant can own, control, or force-sell company assets. Complete protection of business infrastructure.
Equity Incentives Are Temporary
Work-for-shares model is designed as a bootstrap mechanism, not a permanent structure. Eventually transitions to traditional employment.
System Must Reach Traditional Structure
Once stability is achieved, the system transitions to fixed salaries, bonuses, and limited ESOPs with full transparency.
System Philosophy
The FCP system creates a founder-safe, incentive-driven company framework that can be cleanly coded, audited, scaled, and explained — enabling real value creation without compromising control, assets, or long-term business health. It acknowledges that bootstrapping requires sacrifice, but ensures that sacrifice is temporary, measured, and leads to a sustainable traditional business structure.
